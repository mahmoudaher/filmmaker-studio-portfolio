Syrian White Helmets Work Miracles After Earthquake

In the aftermath of the devastating earthquake that struck Turkey and northern Syria, the Syrian White Helmets emerged once again as a symbol of resilience and humanity.

Working through rubble, freezing nights, and unimaginable loss, these volunteer rescuers risked everything to save lives — offering hope in a place overwhelmed by tragedy.

For many survivors, the White Helmets were the difference between death and life, proving that even in the darkest disasters, compassion can still shine.
